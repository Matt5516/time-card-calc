Decimal hours converter

8:15 is 8.25 hours. Convert either way.

How it works: Decimal hours = hours + minutes ÷ 60. To go back, multiply the decimal part by 60 to get minutes.

The short answer

8 hours 15 minutes = 8.25 decimal hours, because 15 ÷ 60 = 0.25. Going back, 7.75 hours is 7 hours and 0.75 × 60 = 45 minutes, or 7:45.

Quick reference

0.25 = 15 minutes, 0.5 = 30 minutes, 0.75 = 45 minutes. Every minute is 0.0167 hours, which is why 10 minutes is 0.17 (rounded) and 20 minutes is 0.33. Most payroll systems round to 2 decimals.

FAQ

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours; 20 minutes is 0.33 hours.

How do I convert decimal hours to minutes?

Multiply the decimal part by 60. 0.4 hours is 24 minutes, so 6.4 hours is 6:24.
