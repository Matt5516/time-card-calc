$30,000 to $150,000 a year, as an hourly rate before taxes.

Divide the salary by 2,080 (40 hours × 52 weeks). $50,000 a year is $24.04 an hour, $75,000 is $36.06 and $100,000 is $48.08. All figures are gross, before taxes.

FAQ

How do I convert a salary to an hourly rate?

Divide the annual salary by 2,080, the hours in a 40-hour, 52-week year.

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

$24.04 an hour before taxes.

How much is $100,000 a year per hour?

$48.08 an hour before taxes.
