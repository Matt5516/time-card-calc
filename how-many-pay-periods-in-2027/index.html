How many pay periods in 2027?

Pick your payday and frequency to list every 2027 payday.

How it works: Paydays are counted from the calendar: weekly and biweekly paydays repeat every 7 or 14 days from a payday you pick; semimonthly is the 15th and the last day; monthly is the last day.

The short answer

2027 has 26 biweekly pay periods, or 27 if your first payday is Friday, January 1; 52 weekly (53 if you are paid on Fridays); 24 semimonthly; and 12 monthly. Weekly and biweekly counts depend on your first payday, so use the tool to list yours.

What about 2026?

The same math for 2026 (January 1, 2026 is a Thursday): weekly paydays on Thursdays number 53, other weekdays 52; biweekly paydays number 27 if your first 2026 payday was Thursday, January 1, and 26 otherwise. Switch the tool above to 2026 to list yours.

What a 27th paycheck means

An extra pay period in the year does not mean extra annual pay by itself. Salaried employees are usually paid the same annual salary, split by the employer into 26 (or 27) equal checks, and hourly employees are paid for hours worked in each period, so the extra check just covers a shorter share of the year. Check with your employer or payroll provider how it is handled. To turn an hourly rate into paychecks see the hourly to salary calculator, and for a two-week total of hours use the biweekly timesheet or the time card calculator.

FAQ

How many biweekly pay periods are in 2027?

26 for most people. It is 27 only if your first 2027 payday is Friday, January 1, 2027, because 26 two-week intervals after that land on December 31.

How many weekly pay periods are in 2027?

52, or 53 if you are paid on Fridays: 2027 has 365 days (52 weeks and one day) and January 1, 2027 is a Friday.

Which months have 3 paychecks in 2027 if I am paid biweekly?

It depends on your first payday. If it is Friday, January 1: January, July and December. If it is Friday, January 8: April and October. The tables above list every possible start.

How many semimonthly pay periods are in 2027?

24: the 15th and the last day of each month. Semimonthly is different from biweekly, which has 26.

How many pay periods are in a year?

52 weekly (sometimes 53), 26 biweekly (sometimes 27), 24 semimonthly and 12 monthly.
