The short answer

Military time is the 24-hour clock written without a colon: 1:00 PM is 1300, 5:30 PM is 1730 and midnight is 0000. For PM times, add 12 to the hour; AM times keep their hour, with a leading zero. To go back, subtract 12 from any hour over 12.

How to convert

  1. AM: keep the hour and pad to two digits: 9:05 AM = 0905. 12:xx AM becomes 00xx (12:15 AM = 0015).
  2. PM: add 12 to the hour: 5:30 PM = 17:30. 12:xx PM stays 12xx.
  3. Back to 12-hour: hours 13–23 subtract 12 and add PM; 00 becomes 12 AM; 12 is PM.

How military time is said

Hours are read as two digits, then “hundred” for the top of the hour: 0600 is “zero six hundred”, 1730 is “seventeen thirty” and 0905 is “zero nine zero five”. The word “hours” follows ("fifteen hundred hours"). 0000 is often said “zero zero hundred” or “zero hundred”.

FAQ

How do I convert 12-hour time to military time?

For AM, keep the hour (add a leading zero, 9:05 AM = 0905); 12 AM becomes 00. For PM, add 12 to the hour (5:30 PM = 1730); 12 PM stays 12.

Is 2400 a valid time?

Some systems write 2400 for the end of a day, but 0000 is the standard way to write midnight. The converter reads 2400 as midnight.
