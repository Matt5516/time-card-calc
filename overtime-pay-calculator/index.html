Overtime pay calculator

Hours and a rate in. Gross pay out.

How it works: Regular pay = regular hours × rate. Overtime pay = overtime hours × rate × 1.5. Double time = hours × rate × 2. Multipliers are editable.

Hours and rate

Decimal (7.5) or h:mm (7:30).

The short answer

At $20 an hour, 40 regular hours and 5 overtime hours pay $800.00 + 5 × $30.00 = $950.00 gross. Overtime at time-and-a-half is $30.00 an hour.

The federal Fair Labor Standards Act requires covered, nonexempt employees to be paid at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. See the U.S. Department of Labor, Fact Sheet #23. State rules, such as daily overtime in California, can add more. This is not legal advice.

FAQ

How do I calculate overtime pay?

Multiply your hourly rate by 1.5 and by the overtime hours, then add the regular pay. $20 × 1.5 × 5 = $150 on top of 40 × $20 = $800.

Is the rate used for overtime always the base rate?

The law uses the "regular rate", which can include things like some bonuses. This calculator uses the rate you enter. Ask your employer or the DOL if you are unsure.
