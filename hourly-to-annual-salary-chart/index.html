Hourly to annual salary chart

$10 to $50 an hour, before taxes.

$20 an hour is $41,600 a year ($3,467 a month, $1,600 every two weeks) at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. $25 an hour is $52,000. The shortcut: double the hourly rate and add three zeros gives a close estimate (a full-time year is 2,080 hours). All figures are gross, before taxes and deductions.

The formula

Annual = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year Full time = 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours Monthly = annual ÷ 12 Biweekly = annual ÷ 26 Weekly = annual ÷ 52 Hourly = annual salary ÷ 2,080

Pre-tax only. Take-home pay is lower after federal and state income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and benefit deductions. Overtime is not included; use the hourly to salary calculator to add overtime hours, change hours per week and weeks per year, or convert a salary back to hourly. For the weekly pay from a time card, see the overtime pay calculator.
