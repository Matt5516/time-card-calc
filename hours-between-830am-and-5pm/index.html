Hours between 8:30 AM and 5 PM

Total time, minus lunch, and what it adds up to in a week.

8:30 AM to 5 PM is 8:30 hours (8.50 decimal). With a 30-minute unpaid lunch it is 8:00 (8.00), and with a 1-hour lunch 7:30 (7.50). Five 30-minute-lunch shifts add up to 40:00.

FAQ

How many hours is 8:30 AM to 5 PM?

8:30 hours (8.50 in decimal), with no break subtracted.

How many hours is 8:30 AM to 5 PM with a 30-minute lunch?

8:00 (8.00 hours).

How many hours is 8:30 AM to 5 PM with a 1-hour lunch?

7:30 (7.50 hours).

How many hours is 8:30 AM to 5 PM five days a week?

With a 30-minute unpaid lunch, five shifts total 40:00 (40.00 hours).
