$80,000 a year is how much an hour?

Gross hourly rate before taxes, from 2,080 working hours a year.

$80,000 a year is $38.46 an hour at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks (2,080 hours). That is $6,666.67 a month, $3,076.92 every two weeks and $1,538.46 a week, all gross, before taxes.

The math

$80,000 ÷ (40 hours × 52 weeks) = $80,000 ÷ 2,080 = $38.46 an hour

A quick check: take the salary in thousands and halve it. 80k halved is about 40, close to $38.46.

A salary is the same money for more or fewer hours, so the hourly rate changes with your schedule. At 45 hours a week the same $80,000 works out to $34.19 an hour in straight-time terms, which is why salaried people who work long weeks often feel underpaid. Whether a salaried employee is owed overtime depends on exempt status under the FLSA, not just on pay.

For comparison, the federal minimum wage of $7.25 an hour (U.S. DOL) is $15,080 a year full time; $80,000 is 5.3 times that. Figures are gross: taxes and benefit deductions reduce take-home pay.
