# ClockInMath

Free time card calculator. Enter in and out times to get daily and weekly hours (h:mm and decimal), overtime, gross pay and a printable timesheet. No signup.

**Live site: [https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/)** (free, no signup, runs in your browser)

## Tools
- [Weekly time card calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/time-card-calculator/): Enter in and out times for the week: daily and weekly hours (h:mm and decimal), overtime and gross pay. Print it.
- [Hours between two times](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/hours-between-times/): How many hours and minutes between a start and an end time, with a break and overnight shifts.
- [Decimal hours converter](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/decimal-hours-converter/): Convert hours and minutes to decimal hours and back, with a quick reference table.
- [Overtime pay calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/overtime-pay-calculator/): Regular, overtime and double-time hours at your rate: the gross pay line by line.
- [Biweekly timesheet](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/biweekly-timesheet/): Two weeks of in and out times, with overtime tested week by week and period totals.
- [Military time converter](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/military-time-converter/): 12-hour to 24-hour time and back, with a full 24-hour chart.
- [Pay periods in 2027](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/how-many-pay-periods-in-2027/): List every 2027 payday for weekly, biweekly, semimonthly or monthly pay, plus 3-paycheck months.
- [Hourly to salary calculator](https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/hourly-to-salary-calculator/): Hourly pay to annual, monthly, biweekly and weekly, and back. Pre-tax.

## Data sources
Hours are worked out from the clock times you type (out minus in, minus unpaid breaks, overnight shifts roll into the next day). Overtime rules follow the U.S. Department of Labor's Fact Sheet #23 (FLSA: time-and-one-half after 40 hours in a workweek) and the California Department of Industrial Relations overtime FAQ (daily overtime and double time). Your hours, rate and the overtime rules that apply to you are your own inputs; state and local laws, union contracts and employer policies can differ. ClockInMath is currently served at matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc; clockinmath.com is the planned domain, pending the owner's approval.

## About this repo
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages: no backend, no ads, no cookies (cookieless GoatCounter visit counts only). The math lives in `assets/calc.js` and is covered by automated tests. Pages are generated by a shared build script, so the HTML here should not be edited by hand. Feedback and corrections: [open an issue](https://github.com/Matt5516/time-card-calc/issues).

Estimates only, not legal, tax or payroll advice. Overtime rules vary by state, employer and job; check with your employer or your state labor department. Gross pay is before taxes and deductions.
