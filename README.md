
# ClockInMath

Free time card calculator: weekly and biweekly hours (h:mm and decimal), overtime (weekly over 40, daily over 8 and 12, or both), gross pay and a printable timesheet. Also hours between two times, a decimal hours converter and an overtime pay calculator.

Live: https://matt5516.github.io/time-card-calc/ (planned domain clockinmath.com, pending the owner's approval)

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, no ads, no trackers. Math lives in `assets/calc.js` (tested). Pages are generated by the shared build in Matt's sites workspace, so don't edit the HTML by hand. Not legal, tax or payroll advice.
